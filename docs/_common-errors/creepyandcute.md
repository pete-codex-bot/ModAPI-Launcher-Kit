---
title: Creepy & Cute content missing in-game
description: Instructions for when Creepy & Cute content is missing in-game, preventing use of the parts in the Creature Editor or viewing creatures that use the parts.
---
This is for users who own Creepy & Cute and have it installed, but cannot access the parts in the Creature Editor.

Make sure you are checking the full Creature Editor - from the galaxy menu, choose Create > Creatures > Creature Creator

If you see the red Creepy & Cute icon in the part palette, you do *not* need to follow these steps.

## Steam users
**IMPORTANT: Do NOT install Spore or Creepy & Cute from EA App**
- In your Steam Library, right-click Spore Galactic Adventures > Manage > Browse local files
- Go up one folder, you should see a folder called `spore 17440`, go into it
- You should see a `bp1content` folder, copy this folder
- Go back twice to return to the original folder (may be named `spore24720`)
- Paste `bp1content` here (you should now have `bp1content`, `Data`, `DataEP1`, `SporeBin`, `SporebinEP1` and maybe more)
Only launch the **ModAPI Launcher** to play the game. All parts should be available in the full Creature Editor.

## EA App users
- Open the folder where the game is installed (default `C:\Program Files\EA Games\Spore`)
- Go into the `BP1Data` folder, you should see `BoosterPack_01.package`, copy this file
- Go back the previous folder, go into the `Data` folder, paste the `BoosterPack_01.package` file here
- Go back the previous folder again, go into the `SPORE Galactic Adventures` folder, go into the `Data` folder, and paste the `BoosterPack_01.package` file here too

There are rare instances where Creepy & Cute will stop working entirely for EA App users, even with the above fix. In these cases, the only option is to reinstall the game.

---

## Creepy & Cute content on Spore.com
If Creepy & Cute content is working *in-game*, but you are missing the Creepy & Cute badge on your Spore.com profile and creatures made with the parts or paints do not appear on Spore.com, this means your EA account is missing the entitlement for Creepy & Cute.

This is *not* a game issue. The above instructions will not resolve this, nor will reinstalling.

To obtain an entitlement on your EA account, you must submit proof-of-purchase, either by redeeming a CD key on EA.com (for GOG or disc users), or contacting EA support with a purchase receipt (for Steam users). Please see the [Spore Official Discord server](https://discord.gg/sporeofficial) for more information.

**IMPORTANT: Do NOT install Spore or Creepy & Cute from EA App if it is already installed from GOG or Steam.** The entitlement simply needs to be on your EA account, and will appear as a badge on your Spore.com profile.
